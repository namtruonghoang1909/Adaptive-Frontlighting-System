Road sectors map to high-beam matrix columns.

The 10-sector road grid projects onto eight columns on each headlight. Rows 5–8 stay on as a short, wide low beam. Centered high-beam mode adds rows 1–4 and masks their pixels around vehicles. The demo's example distances 30/50/70/100 m mask 4/3/2/1 top rows in affected columns.

Object X/Y→Sector distances→ADB pixel mask

Column colors identify channels in the map; the matrix pixels show the proposed white output. MAX7219 brightness applies to the whole matrix, while ADB switches individual high-beam pixels off. The default high beam reaches 100 m at ±10°; low beam reaches 40 m at ±18° and swivels up to ±6° at low speed. The outside matrix turns about three times farther than the inside matrix; at high speed both swivel less. These spans, the speed rule, and the distance-based vertical row mask are illustrative and need calibration. The default 10-sector grid follows the provisional 0x300 proposal; other sector counts are exploratory. Override numeric values with AFS_BEAM_CONFIG or URL query values such as ?maxRangeM=300&sectorCount=31.
